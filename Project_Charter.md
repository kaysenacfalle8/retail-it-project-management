# Project Charter — Retail Inventory System Rollout
**Simulation / illustrative only**

## Business need
Separate store and warehouse spreadsheets create a risk of mismatched stock quantities, manual reconciliation, and inaccurate e-commerce availability.

## Objective
Plan a controlled implementation of a centralized inventory system for one pilot retail store and a warehouse, with a defined expansion decision after pilot validation.

## Success criteria (targets, not measured outcomes)
- 100% of pilot SKUs have mapped identifiers and owners before cutover.
- At least 95% of priority user acceptance tests pass before go/no-go review.
- All critical defects are resolved or formally accepted before launch.
- 100% of pilot staff complete training before cutover.
- Inventory reconciliation differences are investigated and documented during pilot.

## Scope
**In:** requirements, inventory data mapping, receiving and stock adjustment workflows, stock alerts, basic e-commerce synchronization testing, pilot, training, cutover and stabilization.

**Out:** procurement of a real platform, production API integrations, payment processing, nationwide deployment, and live customer data.

## Stakeholders and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor | Scope approval, funding assumptions, go/no-go decisions |
| Project Manager | Schedule, meetings, action log, risks, weekly reporting |
| IT Lead | Configuration, integrations, rollback plan |
| Business Analyst | Requirements, SKU mapping, data validation |
| QA Lead | Test cases, defects, test sign-off |
| Store Manager | Training, pilot feedback, adoption |

## Phases
1. Discovery and charter approval
2. Requirements and design
3. Configuration and migration preparation
4. Testing and defect resolution
5. Pilot, readiness review, rollout and stabilization

## Assumptions and constraints
- Dates, owners and statuses are synthetic planning examples.
- A sample environment and fabricated inventory records are available.
- The sponsor reviews critical changes within two working days.
- Any production rollout would require privacy, security, accessibility, and vendor review.

## Communication plan
Weekly project status to sponsor; twice-weekly implementation check-ins; decision log for scope changes; immediate escalation for critical cutover or data-integrity issues.
