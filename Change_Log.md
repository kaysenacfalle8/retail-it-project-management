## Change review process
1. Record request, business justification, requester, and date.
2. Estimate scope, schedule, cost, data privacy, and testing impact.
3. Propose accept, defer, or reject with rationale.
4. Obtain sponsor decision; record it and communicate to affected owners.
5. Update baseline milestones, risks, and task tracker if approved.
